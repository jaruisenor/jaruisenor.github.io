Privacy policy

This policy describes Google account data processed by Oscar Hermes, a personal assistant operated by jaruisenor. It is not a public service. This information website is separate from the private assistant and does not itself request Google access.

Data accessed

With the account owner's Google OAuth authorisation, the assistant may access:

  • Gmail: message and thread content, headers, participants, labels and relevant attachments within the operator's approved review scope.
  • Google Calendar: calendar identity and event information, including dates, times, descriptions and participants, as needed for personal planning.
  • Basic account identity: the account's email address and profile information needed to identify the authorised account.

The current Google scopes are Gmail read-only, Calendar read-only and basic identity. They do not grant sending, deletion or calendar-editing capabilities. The assistant must not expand to another account or purpose without authorisation.

Purpose and use

Data is used to provide requested personal assistance: understanding email replies, identifying possible actions, preparing summaries and drafts, checking commitments, and maintaining owner-approved context. Recurring checks require a separate operating decision; granting OAuth access does not itself enable them. Google account data is not sold or used for advertising.

AI processing and transfers

Selected Google account content may be included in requests to the AI model providers configured for Hermes Agent. Those providers therefore may receive the content needed for an assistant task. This is not a promise that all processing stays on the operator's computer. Provider processing, retention and applicable privacy controls are governed by the provider's terms and the operator's configuration.

Oscar Hermes's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The operator is responsible for selecting processing arrangements consistent with these requirements. This policy does not grant permission for unrelated use or unrestricted disclosure of Google data.

Storage and protection

Google OAuth credentials are kept in the native Google Workspace CLI credential store using encrypted storage. Selected email or calendar content may also remain in private assistant conversation history, task evidence files and approved knowledge notes, including GBrain. Those data files and any backups are controlled by the operator. Encryption of OAuth credentials is not a claim that every note, log or backup is encrypted.

No credentials, email content, calendar records or private knowledge notes are intentionally published in this website's repository. The public site has no Google API client code, advertising or analytics scripts. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs under its own privacy policy.

Retention and deletion

Private assistant records are retained until the operator deletes them or changes the applicable retention settings. The operator can remove notes, conversation records, evidence files and backups through the tools that manage those stores. Deleting one copy does not necessarily remove other copies or backups.

Google access can be revoked through Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorised access but does not automatically erase previously retained assistant records. Revoking access is not a deletion of the original messages or events in Google.

Control and contact

The operator controls which Google account is connected, the scope of review and any permitted actions. The support email is shown on the Google consent screen. Contact the operator privately about access or deletion requests; do not post account data, credentials or sensitive information in public GitHub issues.

Changes

This policy must be updated if the assistant's Google access, processing providers, storage arrangements or purposes materially change. The public repository's version history records revisions.
