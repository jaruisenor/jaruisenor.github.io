Oscar Hermes

A personal household assistant that helps its operator find what needs attention, understand replies and prepare useful next steps.

Oscar runs on Hermes Agent. It is operated for personal use by the GitHub account jaruisenor. It is not a publicly available email service, and this website does not offer account registration or a Google sign-in flow.

What it does

  • Reviews relevant personal email threads to surface replies and potential actions.
  • Checks personal calendar information for upcoming commitments and scheduling context.
  • Uses conversation history and owner-selected notes to avoid repeating already handled items.
  • Prepares concise summaries and drafts for the operator to review.

Google account access

The Google integration currently requests read-only Gmail and Google Calendar permissions, plus basic account identity. The operator authorises access directly with Google. These permissions do not allow Oscar to send or delete Gmail messages or create, edit or delete calendar events.

Read access is not the same as automatically importing every message. The operator determines the review scope and whether any recurring checks are enabled.

Where processing happens

The assistant runs in the operator's environment. Relevant information may be processed by configured AI providers and retained in private conversation history or approved notes. Processing is not necessarily local-only. See the privacy policy for the data flow and retention details.

This public site contains app information only. It does not access or display Google account data and includes no analytics or advertising scripts.
