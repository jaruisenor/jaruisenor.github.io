Personal use

Oscar Hermes is a private personal-assistant configuration operated by jaruisenor. It is not offered as a public subscription product. These pages provide information about that configuration; they do not provide public access to the assistant.

Authorisation and scope

Only connect accounts you own or are authorised to use. Google account access is subject to Google's OAuth consent and policies. The current Gmail and Calendar integration is read-only. Sending communications, modifying accounts or events, bookings and payments require separate operator approval and the appropriate capabilities.

Accuracy and availability

Assistant output can be incomplete, outdated or incorrect. Check original messages, documents and service records before acting on consequential information. The assistant is not a substitute for professional medical, legal or financial advice. Availability depends on the operator's environment and external services; continuous operation is not guaranteed.

Data and control

Read the privacy policy for access, processing, retention and revocation details. The operator can stop using the assistant and revoke Google access. Previously retained local records and backups require separate management.

Contact and changes
